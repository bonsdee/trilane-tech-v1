Email signatures for the Trilane Tech team

Three versions for each of you. Pick your name, check your details, then copy the version you need into Gmail.

1Put the images online

  1. Jivonz copies the email folder from the image pack into frontend/public/ in the trilane-tech project.
  2. Push to GitHub so Cloudflare Pages redeploys.
  3. Check trilanetech.com/email/jl.jpg and /logo.png open in a browser.

Do this before anyone installs a signature. Until then the photo and logo show as blank boxes.

2Test deliverability

  1. Open mail-tester.com and copy the test address it shows.
  2. From Gmail, send it a realistic cold email with the no-photo signature. Check the score.
  3. Repeat with the with photo version and compare.

Also look at the DKIM result: your domain has two Google DKIM keys, and the test shows whether that causes a failure.

3Add it to Gmail

  1. Copy a signature with Copy signature.
  2. In Gmail: gear icon → See all settings → General → Signature → Create new. Name it, e.g. "Cold" or "Client".
  3. Click in the box, press Ctrl+V (Cmd+V on Mac).
  4. Under Signature defaults, pick "Cold" for new emails, and tick the box to insert it before quoted text in replies.
  5. Scroll down, click Save Changes.

You can switch signatures per email with the pen icon at the bottom of a compose window.
